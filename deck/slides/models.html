No. 04

Why run a 27-billion-parameter model when trillion-parameter ones are a click away? Here is the field on the same independent test. One thing first: by late 2026 most vendors stopped publishing SWE-bench Verified, so the common yardstick now is Terminal-Bench, and these are Artificial Analysis's own runs so the harness is the same for everyone. On Terminal-Bench 2.1, Qwen3.8-27B scores about 80, in the same band as GLM-5.3 at 753 billion parameters, and far above the other open models that actually fit in 96 gigabytes: Qwen's own Coder-Next at 38 and gpt-oss-120b at 26. That is why it is the local pick. Be honest about the ceiling, though: on long, hard, many-step tasks the gap to the frontier models is real, and that is why the rest of this talk is about small steps, tests and plans. For one hard step, a hosted model like GLM-5.3 is a reasonable escape hatch.
