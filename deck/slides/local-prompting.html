No. 17

A few more habits that suit a 27-billion-parameter model. Plan in a file: my plan template writes PLAN.md and stops, I read and correct it, and because it is a file it survives compaction and new sessions. Give every task one finish line the model can check, like a named test file passing and the linter clean, never "improve parsing". Keep steps small enough to finish in a few minutes, and chain them through the plan. Leave thinking on medium; drop to low for mechanical edits, but never turn it off: with thinking off, this model told me 9.11 is larger than 9.9. When one step stalls, escalate just that step to a hosted model through slash model, then switch back; I have Hugging Face entries set up for that, though they still need a token and I have not needed them much yet, and remember the switch costs a cold read. And batch: start several tasks in separate clones, go do something else, and review the diffs in one sitting. That is where a slow local agent earns its keep.
